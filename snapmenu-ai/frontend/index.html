How it works

1

Photograph the menu

Upload a picture of your existing paper menu from the owner dashboard.

2

AI structures it

OCR + an LLM extract every dish, price and category — you review and approve.

3

Print the QR codes

Each table gets its own QR. Diners scan, browse a gorgeous menu and order.

4

Kitchen sees it live

Orders stream to the kitchen board in real time — no refresh, no paper tickets.
